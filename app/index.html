Sounds

On iPhone, turn the volume up with the side buttons. Sounds play even with the silent switch on in iOS 17 and later.

Install as an app

Add the timer to your home screen and it opens full screen like a regular app, and works with no signal at the park. Open it once while online and everything is saved on your phone.

You're running the installed app. You're all set!

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button . On iOS 26 it's under the ••• button at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.

Chrome on iPhone works too: tap the Share button in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Install app button above, or tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen, then Install.

Samsung Internet: tap the menu, then Add page to, then Home screen.

During the workout

  • The screen stays awake while the timer runs.
  • Keep the timer on screen. Phones pause web apps that are in the background, so switching apps or locking the phone stops the sounds.
  • Change times and sounds with the gear button. Your choices are remembered.
